Privacy Policy

How Mehewara handles your data

Last updated: June 2025
Overview

Introduction

Mehewara (mehewara.edu.lk) is a free exam practice platform for Sri Lankan O/L and A/L students. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being fully transparent about how we handle data.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use Mehewara, how we use it, and your rights as a user.

Data Collection

Information We Collect

No account required. Mehewara does not require registration or login. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any personally identifiable information.

We may automatically collect the following non-personal data:

  • Browser type and version — for compatibility
  • IP address — processed by Cloudflare for security purposes only
  • Pages visited and links clicked — anonymous usage data
  • Device type — mobile or desktop
  • Country or region — for regional usage insights
Data Use

How We Use Your Data

Any data collected is used solely to:

  • Improve the platform's performance and user experience
  • Identify popular subjects to prioritize new quiz content
  • Maintain security and speed via Cloudflare
  • Manage search visibility via Google Search Console

We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with advertisers or any third-party marketing services.

Cookies

Cookies & Local Storage

Mehewara uses only essential cookies and local storage:

  • Cloudflare cookies — required for security and performance, e.g. __cf_bm
  • Theme preference — stores your dark/light mode selection in localStorage
  • Quiz session — temporarily tracks quiz progress during your session; cleared when you close the browser

We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any third-party tracking cookies.

Third Parties

Third-Party Services

Cloudflare — Mehewara is hosted and protected by Cloudflare. Cloudflare may process your IP address and traffic data for DDoS protection and performance optimization. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

Google Search Console — We use GSC to monitor search indexing. No user personal data is shared with Google through this tool.

KaTeX CDN — Math equations are rendered using KaTeX loaded from cdn.jsdelivr.net. No personal data is transmitted to this service.

Minors

Children's Privacy

Mehewara is designed for students of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child's data has been collected through our platform, please contact us and we will promptly address the concern.

Your Rights

User Rights

Since we do not collect personally identifiable information, there is generally no personal data to access, modify, or delete. If you have any concerns about data related to your usage, you may contact us at any time and we will respond within 7 business days.

Updates

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
